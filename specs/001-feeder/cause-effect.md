# F01 cause-and-effect matrix

**Scope.** Distributed classroom bay SS-01 / F01. PR-F01 decides protection. BRF01 owns arming and lockout. The breaker actuator owns movement and contacts when the process I/O service is introduced. Until then, the BRF01 bench actuator provides independent simulated contacts. These are proposed lab rules and acceptance criteria.

## Common rules

- Protection trips take priority over close at the actuator. Inhibit closing while protection data, authority, process state or confirmed contacts are unavailable or invalid. A validated trip can still request opening when position is unknown, provided its actuation path is available.
- Fault clearance is explicit and owned by the fictional plant scenario. In P1, a declared fixture provides a positive clearance indication. In P2, it comes from the plant. Opening the breaker, zero feeder current, and deasserted pickup do not constitute clearance. An authorized local reset must also see fresh deasserted protection and confirmed OPEN. Clearing the latch leaves the breaker open.
- A service restart differs from a whole-exercise initialization. Report UNKNOWN until feedback is reconciled, or STALE if a persisted sample is available. Recover lockout or apply conservative close inhibit. A first fresh asserted trip must open a confirmed closed breaker even when the earlier clear was never observed. An already confirmed open breaker needs its latch enforced without a redundant open request. A heartbeat or cleared trip cannot unlock or close the breaker.
- Commands have separate **accepted**, **completed**, **superseded**, **rejected** and **unknown outcome** indications. Confirmed position comes from contacts. Preserve last good values as stale when their source is unavailable, alongside live indications and alarms from available sources.
